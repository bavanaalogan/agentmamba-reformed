# Ladle care manual, melt shop SMS1

This manual describes routine care for the steel ladles and their auxiliaries. It is general guidance and does not record any specific event.

When a ladle trips or is removed from service, the root cause must be confirmed by refractory engineering before it is recorded as established. Until then, operators note observations only.

Return to service after repair requires preheating, a functional test of the slide gate and a supervisor signature; a ladle is cleared for operation only by that signed record.
